# From Implementator to Manager



Even in the architecture career there's a limit of how much you can grow and how much complexity a system can have. Even the most complicated project is broken down in pieces a team of professionals can handle.

One of the biggest progressions in my career was passing from Solution Architecture to Talent Manager. The TM role is an extra mile, I am still accountable for the architecture in my projects, I do have to join Daily Stand Ups and drawing diagrams as usual.

**Being a TM requires a complete different mindset, the problems are not technical but human.**

Most people don't to enroll on an extra mile activity to instead work on their individual careers or use the time for anything else. That's also fine, not everyone has the human skills to make others grow, and it's not their fault not to have them but it's a decision not to nurturing them.
